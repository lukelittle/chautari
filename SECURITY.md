# Security policy

Chautari sits in the path of every AI request an organisation makes, so we
take reports seriously.

**Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.** Use GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting ("Security" tab → "Report a vulnerability").
Expect an acknowledgement within three working days and a fix or mitigation
plan within fourteen.

In scope: policy, guardrail, budget or authentication bypasses; audit-log
tampering that verification does not detect; secret or PII disclosure; the
generated Helm, Terraform and Compose artefacts.

Known limitations, documented rather than hidden, are listed under "Status"
in the README.
